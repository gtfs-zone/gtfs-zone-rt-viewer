GTFS Realtime visualizer

viz.rt.gtfs.zone shows a GTFS Realtime feed on a live map. Every feed is fetched and decoded in your browser. Nothing you load is uploaded anywhere.

  • Load a feed: point it at a scheduled GTFS feed plus its realtime feeds.
  • Watch vehicles move: vehicle positions update every few seconds on the map.
  • Check predictions: trip updates show arrival predictions and how late each trip is running.
  • Spot disruptions: active service alerts show up alongside the routes.

Part of gtfs.zone, open source tools for GTFS and GTFS Realtime. See the GTFS Realtime reference.
